# Station fact check, pass 3, 2026-10-05

Pass 3 is the held-out test of the ranker. It reviewed every pair of the frozen holdout (`apps/web/scripts/facts/lab/holdout-pass3.json`), and then measured the `facts-2` and `facts-3` risks that the holdout stored before the review. Pass 1 is in [2026-10-05-fact-check.md](2026-10-05-fact-check.md) and pass 2 in [2026-10-05-fact-check-pass2.md](2026-10-05-fact-check-pass2.md). This report does not change the copy: the fixes are listed for a later commit.

## Summary

- 712 pairs reviewed, all the holdout pairs. No holdout pair is without a verdict.
- Verdicts: 667 supported, 26 confirmed errors, 18 true but unsourced, 1 flag refuted. Problems (confirmed or unsourced): 44 (6.2%).
- On this held-out set, neither ranker is useful for finding errors. Problem AP is 0.125 (`facts-2`) and 0.120 (`facts-3`), against a base rate of 0.062. AUC is 0.747 and 0.735. In the top 20, `facts-3` has 1 confirmed error and `facts-2` has less than 1. Recall@100 of confirmed errors is 0.30 (`facts-2`) and 0.23 (`facts-3`).
- `facts-3` does not beat `facts-2`: the paired bootstrap gives P(AP `facts-3` > AP `facts-2`) = 0.38. The dev and val gains of `facts-3` came mostly from planted errors, and they do not carry over to real errors.
- 10 of the 26 confirmed errors (38%) are in categories that a support question cannot see: 7 overstated wording and 3 EN/FR mismatches.
- Errors are not even across lines: Line 6 has 11 of the 26, Line 1 has 7.

## Method

### Holdout

`lab/holdout-pass3.json` was frozen at commit `82f0aeb` (written 2026-10-05 15:35 UTC, committed as `b6984d4`), before any pass-3 verdict existed. It has every ranked pair of the run `out/pass3` (877 pairs, Jev `jev-1.13.0`, questions `facts-2`) that passes 1 and 2 did not review, less the two Saint-Mandé known conflicts:

| Status | Pairs |
|---|---|
| Never reviewed | 681 |
| Changed materially after review (new detail) | 13 |
| New sentence in a field that pass 1 or 2 fixed | 18 |
| Total | 712 |

For each pair the holdout stores the EN and FR texts, and the risk and rank under two rankers from the same run:

- `facts-3` (production): the mean of the passage and whole-source answers, mean `unsupported` over the pair, `contradicted` 0.2.
- `facts-2` (the baseline): the passage answer only, with `FACTS2_RISK_WEIGHTS`.

### Review

The 712 pairs were cut into 36 batches of about 20 pairs, by line and station. The verifiers saw the pair, its full unit text in EN and FR, and the station's sources. They did not see risks, ranks, flags or review status. Each verifier read the cited sources and other reliable sources, and gave each pair a verdict with a quote. Each flag (a possible error, or a claim not in the cited sources) then went to a second agent that tried to refute it. The final verdicts are the ones after refutation:

- `supported`: a cited source states the claim.
- `confirmed`: the text is wrong or imprecise, and the refutation failed. Each has a category (`misleading`, `overstated-wording`, `wrong-fact`, `wrong-date`, `en-fr-mismatch`) and a proposed fix in both locales.
- `unsourced`: the claim is true, but no cited source states it. Each has a source to add, which the verifier opened (HTTP 200) and quoted.
- `refuted`: flagged, then shown to be correct.

The labels are in `apps/web/scripts/facts/lab/labels/pass3.json` (verdict, category, evidence, fixes and sources) and in `PASS3_REVIEWED` in `apps/web/scripts/facts/reviewed.ts` (verdict, category and texts). `PASS3_REVIEWED` is not in `ALL_REVIEWED`, so the lab's dev and val sets do not change. Its `rank` is the order by the `facts-3` risk in the holdout, not a review order.

### Test

`bun apps/web/scripts/facts/lab/holdout-test.ts` computes the metrics with `lab/metrics.ts`. The positive class is a problem (confirmed or unsourced), against supported or refuted, as in `evaluate.ts`. Ties are one group. Recall@k is for confirmed errors only. The test ran once, after the labels were saved. Nothing was tuned after it.

What this means:

- Both rankers put problems above the median, but not near the top. To find half of the confirmed errors, a reviewer must read about 180 pairs (`facts-3`) or 135 pairs (`facts-2`).
- The rankers find unsourced claims better than errors. This is what the question asks ("does a cited source state this?").
- The errors that a support question can see (wrong fact, wrong date, misleading) do not rank higher than the ones it cannot see. Median position with `facts-3`: 205 for the 16 visible errors, 182 for the 10 overstated or EN/FR errors. Most real errors here are a small change to a fact that the source states (a word added, a scope widened, a tense), and Jev gives these a high `supported`.
- The lab's dev and val sets came from the top of earlier rankings, where problems are frequent (about 40% of the real dev items). The holdout is the rest of the copy, where 6% are problems. The dev gains of `facts-3` came mostly from planted errors. On real labels the two rankers are equal within the noise.
