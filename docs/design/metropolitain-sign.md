# Métropolitain sign replacement

The first code-drawn sign used thin lettering and an invented frame. It was replaced at the user's request with a realistic illustration based on a photographed Guimard entrance sign.

Reference: [01 Guimard's Métropolitain](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:01_Guimard%27s_M%C3%A9tropolitain.jpg), photographed by [Terrazzo](https://www.flickr.com/photos/terrazzo/3959190648/), licensed [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/).

The replacement is an AI-generated adaptation: the photograph's perspective is made frontal; the background, birds, lamps and tall supports are removed. Letter shapes, enamel colour and molded iron details guide the illustration. It is interpretive artwork, not a measured reproduction. The original reference photo is not shipped.

The generated original is retained with the design sources. The site serves a WebP copy with transparency preserved. A reference credit appears in About.

## Generation prompt

Use case: historical-scene. Asset type: realistic isolated website header illustration. Reference image: photograph of a real Hector Guimard METROPOLITAIN sign, use as structural and lettering reference, not as a background. Create a meticulously rendered, front-facing, level, museum-quality illustration of ONLY the complete horizontal METROPOLITAIN enamel sign and its close-fitting cast-iron frame. Precisely follow the reference's characteristic bold sculpted Art Nouveau dark green lettering: eccentric thick organic strokes, large T forms, sweeping R leg, distinctive M and N. Text exactly METROPOLITAIN. Aged warm pale ivory-yellow enamel panel with rounded ends; deep olive green cast iron frame with believable molded botanical ridges and restrained patina. Authentic proportions and dimensional construction. Small historical architect signature may be omitted; no invented text. Orthographic frontal view, not the photograph's perspective tilt. Complete frame visible with modest short mounting flourishes at left and right, no tall posts, no lamps, no trees, no sky, no pigeons, no environment. Realistic fine architectural illustration with tactile enamel and metal, quiet soft daylight, subtle natural highlights and shadows. NOT sketchy, NOT thin hand-lettering, NOT cartoon, NOT childish, NOT simplified vector. Wide composition approx 3:1. Transparent background, actual alpha outside the isolated object. Object fills width with small safe margins, centered vertically and tightly framed. Suitable at 360px wide above an editorial website title.
