The city between
the stops.

Métro Stories is an independent, illustrated history atlas. Start with Line 14, the automated line that first opened in 1998 and now links Saint-Denis–Pleyel with Orly airport.

Every station has a short story and a source to keep exploring. Dates in the stories describe local history; “Line 14 arrived” gives the station’s opening year on this line.

The route is a schematic, not a street map. Illustrations are interpretive. This is not a live journey planner or an official RATP service.
